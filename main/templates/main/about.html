Welcome to Swoo PetMail! 🐾

At Swoo PetMail, we believe that pets aren’t just animals—they’re our family. No matter who lives in your home—a playful puppy, a curious kitten, a chirpy bird, or a little fluffy dwarf hamster—our mission is to make caring for them as simple, enjoyable, and convenient as possible.

Our Story

Swoo PetMail was founded in 2020 by passionate pet owners based on a simple idea: every pet deserves high-quality products, and every owner deserves convenient and reliable service. We started with a small selection of the most essential items, and today we’ve grown into a trusted online store for pet lovers across the country.

Why choose us?

  • 🐶 Care comes first: We offer only products we’d choose for our own furry friends
  • 🚚 Fast and reliable delivery: Convenient delivery right to your door, so you never run out of the most important pet supplies.
  • 💚 Attentive support: Do you have questions or need advice on what to choose? Our team is always ready to help you find the perfect item for your pet.
  • 🔒 Secure shopping: A convenient and secure checkout process for your peace of mind.
